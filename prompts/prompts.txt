NOTE ON PROMPT LANGUAGE
The reported experiments used the Chinese prompts shown below (the team authored
them in Chinese during development). Prompt language can affect LLM outputs
(Behzad et al., 2024), so we ran a controlled Chinese-vs-English comparison on the
core subset; see dataset/experiment_results/language_comparison/. Summary: English
gave a modest overall gain (+6.7pp, paired t p=0.075, n.s. at 0.05) that was highly
model-dependent (Opus +23.8pp; Sonnet and Haiku +/-2pp). Faithful English
translations are provided below.
